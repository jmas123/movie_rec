Binged

Rank what you watch by comparing it to what you have already seen.

Nobody knows what they mean by “7.5 out of 10”. Everybody knows which of two films they would rather watch again. Binged only ever asks the second question.

  1. Mark something watched, and say whether you liked it, it was fine, or you didn’t.
  2. The app asks which of two titles you preferred — a handful of times, never more than it needs.
  3. A 0–10 score falls out of where that lands it. You never type a number, because the number is the answer, not the question.

Coming to iPhone. Films and series are ranked separately, so each gets its own number one.

Works fully without an account, and signed out nothing leaves your phone. An account saves your library so it follows you to a new device — which is a backup, not a broadcast. Sharing is a separate switch and it is off until you turn it on.
