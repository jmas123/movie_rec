Binged — Support

Rank what you watch, by comparing it to what you already have.

Email support@trybinged.com and a person will read it. Tell us which iPhone and which iOS version if something is broken — it saves a round trip.

The things people ask first

  • Why can't I type a score? You don't rate in Binged, you compare. Mark something watched, say whether you liked it, and the app asks which of two titles you preferred a handful of times. The 0–10 number comes out of where that lands it — which is why it means something.
  • Where is my score? A number derived from one or two comparisons would be a guess with a decimal point on it, so until a bucket holds five titles the app shows your position instead, and tells you how many more it needs.
  • Do I need an account? No. Everything — search, ranking, recommendations — works signed out, and signed out nothing leaves your phone. An account saves your library so it follows you to a new device.
  • Who can see my rankings? Nobody, until you turn sharing on. Saving and sharing are separate switches; the privacy policy spells out which is which.
  • Delete my account? Email the address above and we remove the account and everything attached to it.

Reporting someone

Comments and profiles can be reported from inside the app, and you can block an account from its profile. Reports are reviewed and acted on within 24 hours. If something needs faster attention, email us and say so in the subject line.
